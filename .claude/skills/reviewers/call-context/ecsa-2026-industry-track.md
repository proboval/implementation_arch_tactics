# ECSA 2026 Industry Track — Evaluation Context

## Venue

**European Conference on Software Architecture 2026 — Industry Track**
Short Papers and Presentations: up to 8 pages, Springer LNCS style, single-blind peer review.

## Evaluation Criteria (Industry Track Short Papers)

ECSA Industry Track short papers are selected on three criteria:

| Criterion | Weight / Focus |
|---|---|
| **Originality** | Does the paper advance knowledge or practice? Novel ideas, methods, or findings from real industrial use. Simple, elegant solutions are acceptable — complexity is not a proxy for novelty. Replication studies confirming prior findings are acceptable if properly contextualized. |
| **Practical Relevance** | Does the paper speak to practitioners? Does it address a real, recurring industrial problem? Is the guidance actionable? Is the context generalizable beyond the specific case? |
| **Potential for Discussion** | Does the paper raise interesting open questions, surface surprises, or challenge common assumptions? Would it provoke a lively session at a practitioners-and-researchers conference? |

Additionally, all ECSA papers are implicitly evaluated on:

| Criterion | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Soundness** | Is the methodology appropriate and described rigorously? Are claims proportionate to evidence? Are limitations and threats to validity acknowledged? |
| **Quality** | Clear writing, well-organized structure, readable tables/figures, correct LNCS formatting, adequate English. |

## Paper Type: Short Papers and Presentations

- Max 8 pages (including references), Springer LNCS
- Focus: "practices and experience related to software architecture from the industry"
- Not expected to have the full methodology depth of a 16-page research paper
- A clear, honest "what we did and what we learned" structure is appropriate
- Tables summarizing results are explicitly acceptable (no plots required)

## Mandatory Policy Requirements (desk-rejection if missing)

### AI Disclosure (GAIDeT taxonomy)
Every paper using AI tools in research or writing **must** include an Acknowledgements section that:
- Names each AI tool used
- Describes its role using GAIDeT domains: conceptualization, literature review, methodology, code generation, data management, quality assurance, writing, figures/multimedia, supervision, ethical review
- Grammar/spellcheck tools are exempt from disclosure

### Data Availability Statement
Required section immediately after Conclusions. Must either:
- Link to artifacts (anonymous repository preferred for review)
- Explain why artifacts are not available / will not be shared

Sharing is not mandatory but must be explicitly addressed.

### Single-Blind Review
Authors are **not** anonymized. ECSA 2026 Industry Track uses single-blind review. The author list must be present and complete in the submitted PDF.

### Concurrent Submission
Paper must not be under review elsewhere, and must not have been published elsewhere. Plagiarism check: >20% match with published work → desk rejected.

## Scoring Reference

ECSA does not publish a formal 1–5 rubric, but based on the criteria the effective acceptance bar is:
- **Accept:** Strong on ≥2 of (Originality, Practical Relevance, Potential for Discussion) AND no serious soundness or quality failures
- **Borderline:** Notable strength on one criterion, weakness on another — requires discussion
- **Reject:** Fails on Originality (known results, no new angle) OR Practical Relevance (pure academic exercise) OR serious soundness failure

## Formatting Quick Reference (LNCS)

- Page size: A4, single column
- Margins: 4.3 cm top/bottom, 3.3 cm left/right (standard LNCS template)
- Body font: Times 10pt
- Section headings: numbered, bold
- References: numbered, LNCS bibliography style
- Tables/figures: must have captions and be referenced in the text
- Keywords: 4–6 keywords after abstract
