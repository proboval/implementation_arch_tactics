Research Topics

Testing Fundamentals & Techniques
• Test generation and selection techniques
• Test automation and framework design
• Regression testing and test maintenance
• Test adequacy and coverage criteria
• Mutation testing and fault injection

Testing Emerging Technologies
• Testing AI/ML systems and LLMs
• Testing and validation of generative AI outputs
• Verification and testing of autonomous AI agents
• LLM-assisted test case generation and oracle design
• Quantum software testing
• IoT and cyber-physical systems testing
• Blockchain and smart contract testing
• Cloud-native and microservices testing

Formal Methods & Model-Based Testing
• Model-based test generation
• Formal verification and theorem proving
• Model checking and symbolic execution
• Specification-based testing
• Testing and modeling languages

Security & Quality Testing
• Security testing and vulnerability analysis
• Fuzzing and penetration testing
• Adversarial robustness testing of LLMs and generative AI
• Cybersecurity testing for AI-powered systems and agentic workflows
• Performance and reliability testing
• Usability and accessibility testing
• Safety-critical systems testing

Continuous Testing & DevOps
• CI/CD testing strategies
• Test impact analysis and selection
• Shift-left and shift-right testing
• Testing in agile and DevOps environments
• Monitoring and testing in production

Applications & Case Studies
• Mobile and web application testing
• Automotive and embedded systems testing
• Healthcare and medical device testing
• Industrial applications and case studies
• Testing tool development and evaluation
Submission Guidelines
Paper Categories & Format
• Full papers: 12 to 15 pages plus at most 2 extra pages for references.
• Industry papers: 12 to 15 pages plus at most 2 extra pages for references.
• Short or work-in-progress papers: up to 6 pages plus at most 1 extra page for references.
• Journal-First: 4-page extended abstract.
• Format: Springer LNCS (one-column) - Springer Instructions for Authors (includes LaTeX and Word templates)
• File format: PDF only
• Language: English
• Upon acceptance, the corresponding author must sign the Springer licence-to-publish agreement on behalf of all co-authors. The corresponding author cannot be changed after submission to Springer.
• Alternative Text for images is not mandatory. If not provided, Springer typesetters will generate it; the corresponding author will review it during proofing.
Author Requirements (Springer)
• Author names and affiliations must be final at submission. Once a paper is sent to Springer, authors cannot be added, removed, or reordered, and names and affiliations cannot be changed.
• Each paper must designate exactly one corresponding author (with a mandatory email address), clearly marked in the paper header.
• Authors are encouraged to include their ORCID identifiers next to their names (register at orcid.org).
• Accepted papers must include a Disclosure of Interests statement (declare any competing interests, or state that there are none).
• References should follow the Springer/CrossRef style, and authors are strongly encouraged to include DOIs where available.
• See the Instructions for Authors page for full Springer formatting requirements.
Review Process
• All submissions will be peer-reviewed (double-blind)
• Submissions must be anonymized: remove author names and affiliations, and anonymize self-references and any identifying information for review.
• Minimum of 3 reviewers per paper
• Criteria: originality, significance, technical quality, and clarity
Publication
• Accepted papers will be published by Springer LNCS (Lecture Notes in Computer Science).
• Browse all previous ICTSS proceedings on SpringerLink.
• The proceedings will be published approximately 8–10 weeks after receipt of the final manuscript files.
• Conference participants will have 4 weeks of free access to the proceedings via the conference website (subject to activation with Springer).
• Approximately one month after publication, each author will receive a personal access link to download their paper PDF directly from SpringerLink.
• Best papers will be invited to submit an extended version to a special issue of Springer Nature Computer Science.
• At least one author must register and present the paper in person.
• Authors are required to adhere to the Springer Nature Code of Conduct for Book Authors.
